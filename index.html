04 / How we work

Start small.
Make it useful. Build from there.

A clear scope from the start, your team involved at the right moments, and a practical plan for what comes next.

Your starting point

Bring one workflow.
Let’s find a useful next step.

Start with a discovery conversation about a task that takes too much time. You don’t need an AI strategy or a technical brief.

What we’ll explore together
  • How the task works today and how often it happens.
  • The tools involved, the information needed, and where work gets stuck.
  • What should stay with a person and how we could measure improvement.
If there’s a fit

We propose a focused pilot with a written scope, deliverables, price, and timeline for you to review. The call is not a commitment to a build.

What to bring

A description of the task and the tools you use. A fictional or redacted example is enough to start; no passwords or system access are needed for the first conversation.

01

Understand the work

Before a build: an agreed scope and success measures.
02

Build & test together

You get: a pilot and a review against agreed measures.
03

Launch with a plan

After reviewing the pilot, we decide together whether to refine, stop, or roll out. Any rollout includes agreed training, documentation, and support arrangements.

05 / A few good questions

Let’s make
things clear.

You don’t need a detailed technical brief. A task that takes too much time is a good place to start.

Will this work with the tools we already use?

That’s where we start. We check the integrations, access permissions, and data available in your existing tools before recommending a solution. We’ll explain any limits or changes needed before agreeing the scope.

How much does a project cost?

Cost depends on the workflow, integrations, and complexity. After an initial conversation, we define the scope and provide a proposal so you can decide whether the investment makes sense.

How much time will our team need to contribute?

We’ll need someone who understands the workflow to help map it and review the pilot. The time commitment and review points are agreed during scoping.

How will our business data be handled?

Before building, we agree what data is needed, which services will process it, who can access it, and any retention requirements. Those choices are part of the solution design.

Does AI make decisions without us?

We define the boundaries with you. Routine steps can be automated, while important decisions, uncertain results, and sensitive actions can be routed to your team for approval.

What happens after launch?

We document the workflow and help your team use it. Monitoring, maintenance, and future improvements are covered by the support arrangement agreed for your project.
